Increasing the batch size reduces \ourprotocol's preprocessing communication by 19.6\%.
However, its median preprocessing time increases from 15.01 to 16.88\,s locally
and from 23.16 to 26.27\,s on this WAN.
Batcher saves 71.5\% of its preprocessing bytes but also takes longer in both settings.
The larger batches require more peak memory.
Online byte counts and dependency bounds are identical across the two batch sizes.
No process swap was observed in the periodic samples.
These measurements do not support using $2^{22}$-entry batches to improve elapsed time in these settings.
